EVIDENCE RULE

Observed local rotation is real. Nonzero universal cosmic rotation is not an established observation. Similar algebra across fields does not establish one shared physical generator.

Planck's physically coupled Bianchi VIIh analysis found no evidence for that model and reported a model-specific upper limit of (ω/H)₀ < 7.6×10⁻¹⁰ at 95% confidence.

Source: Planck Collaboration, A&A (2016). Constraint is not universal to every possible rotating metric.

What the lab can and cannot show

Independent checks belong in GitHub Actions
The hard null: A smooth real scalar has zero curl of its gradient. A homogeneous complex phase can rotate internally without any spatial winding. A cosmological scalar expansion equation can be degenerate in curvature and angular-speed parameters. These are essential counterexamples to an overly broad universal-rotation assertion.

The vector and fluid experiments illustrate kinematic identities; the Clifford panel illustrates a double-cover representation; and the ME-056 panel displays two mathematically indistinguishable expansion curves. None claims an observational detection, and no field is made to share a cosmic generator without an additional physical derivation.
