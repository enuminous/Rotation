Current scientific standing

Global cosmic rotation is not an established result. Laboratory vortices, spinors, black-hole spin and local frame dragging are observed or well tested. Planck's Bianchi VIIh analysis found no evidence for that physical rotating-cosmology model and placed a model-specific bound of (ω/H)₀ < 7.6×10⁻¹⁰ at 95% confidence. Cross-field analogy is not a measurement.

What would change the conclusion?

A self-consistent rotating Einstein metric and defined observer congruence; a cross-sector physical mechanism with explicit dimensions; a numerical prediction not already explained by conventional physics; and an independently controlled astronomical test that survives Planck and other constraints.
